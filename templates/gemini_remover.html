Good to know

Only the visible logo

This tool cleans the sparkle you can see in the corner. Gemini also embeds an invisible AI marker (SynthID) inside the file — that stays, so the picture remains identifiable as AI-made.

Your photo is not stored

The image is processed in memory and the clean copy is sent straight back to you. Nothing is saved on our server.

Best on Gemini exports

The tool expects the logo in the bottom-right corner, where Gemini puts it. If the corner of your photo holds something important, it will be rebuilt too — check the preview.

Label AI content

Platforms like TikTok and Instagram ask you to label AI-generated content when you post it. A cleaned corner does not change that — please follow each platform's rules.

Frequently asked questions

Is this free?

Yes, completely free — no account, no limits for normal use.

Does it work on Gemini videos?

Not yet. Cleaning a video means cleaning every frame, which needs a much bigger server than this free tool runs on. For now it is photos only.

Will the quality drop?

The photo keeps its original size (very large photos are resized to a 2400px longest edge) and is saved at high quality. Only the corner area is rebuilt.

Is it OK to remove the logo?

Please use it on your own images. Removing the visible logo does not remove your responsibilities: the invisible AI marker remains, and platforms may still require an AI label when you publish.
