Frequently asked questions

Is the SoundCloud downloader free?

Yes. It is free to use with no limits. The site is supported by advertising.

What format will my track be in?

SoundCloud stores tracks in formats such as MP3 and M4A. SnapFetch offers you exactly the formats the track itself provides, so what you download is the original file.

Can I download a whole playlist or album?

This page is made for single tracks. Paste the link of the individual track you want to save.

Can I download private or paid tracks?

No. Only tracks that are publicly playable on SoundCloud can be fetched. Private tracks and tracks the artist has made download-restricted or paid cannot be saved here.

Is it OK to download any track?

Please only download tracks you own, that are in the public domain or shared under a license that allows downloading, or that you have the artist's permission to save — and use them for personal listening. Many artists also enable SoundCloud's own download button; support them where you can. See our Terms of Use.

Where does the downloaded file go?

It saves to your device's Downloads folder, like any other file from your browser.
