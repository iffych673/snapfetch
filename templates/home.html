Frequently asked questions

Is SnapFetch free?

Yes. SnapFetch is free to use, with no limits on how many videos you save. The site is supported by advertising.

Which sites are supported?

TikTok, Facebook (including Reels), Instagram (Reels and posts), YouTube (including Shorts) and X. If a platform changes something, a link may stop working for a while — try again later.

Can I download private videos?

No. SnapFetch can only fetch videos that are publicly viewable. If a video is private or friends-only, it cannot be downloaded here.

Where do downloaded videos go?

They save to your device's Downloads folder, like any other file from your browser.

Is it OK to download any video?

Please only download videos you own, that are in the public domain, or that you have the creator's permission to save, and use them for personal purposes. See our Terms of Use.
