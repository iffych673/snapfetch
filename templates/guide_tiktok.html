How to Download TikTok Videos Without Watermark

TikTok is full of videos worth keeping — a recipe you want to try, a dance tutorial, or one of your own uploads you want backed up somewhere safe. TikTok's built-in save button does not always appear, and saved videos stay inside the app. This guide shows the simplest way to download a TikTok video to your own device using SnapFetch, a free tool that works in any browser — and because SnapFetch fetches the original file, the video saves without the TikTok watermark.

Why downloads from SnapFetch have no watermark

The moving TikTok logo you see on a playing video is added by the app at playback time — it is not part of the video file itself. SnapFetch downloads the original file stored on TikTok's servers, before that logo is added, so the video you save is clean: no bouncing logo, no username stamp. The same trick cannot remove a watermark that a creator burned into a video before uploading — that kind is part of the picture itself.

Step-by-step: download any public TikTok video

  1. Find the video. Open TikTok and go to the video you want to save.
  2. Tap the Share button. It is the arrow icon on the right side of the video.
  3. Choose “Copy link”. The video's web address is now on your clipboard.
  4. Open SnapFetch. Go to the SnapFetch home page in your browser — no app install is needed.
  5. Paste the link into the big box at the top and press Download.
  6. Pick your format. Choose a video quality, or pick MP3 if you only want the sound. The file saves straight to your device.

Downloading on iPhone

On an iPhone, the steps are the same, but where the file lands depends on your browser. In Safari, downloaded videos go to the Files app under Downloads. If you want the video in your Photos library, open it in Files, tap the Share icon, and choose “Save Video”.

Downloading on Android

On Android, Chrome and most other browsers save the file to your Downloads folder. You can open it from the notification that appears when the download finishes, or find it later in your gallery or file manager.

Downloading on a computer

On a PC or Mac, copying the link is even easier: open the TikTok video in your browser and copy the address from the address bar. Paste it into SnapFetch, choose a format, and the file downloads like any other file from the web.

Tips for better results

  • Use the full link. If a shortened link fails, open it once in your browser so it expands to the full video address, then copy that.
  • Pick the quality you need. Higher quality means a bigger file. If you only plan to watch on your phone, a mid-range option is usually plenty.
  • Back up your own videos. Downloading your own uploads is the easiest way to keep an original copy outside the app, before any edits or captions were added in TikTok.
  • Check your storage. A full phone can silently stop downloads. If a download fails for no obvious reason, free up a little space and try again.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download a private TikTok?

No. SnapFetch can only fetch videos that anyone can view publicly. Private and friends-only videos cannot be downloaded.

Will the video really have no watermark?

Yes — as long as the watermark is TikTok's own playback logo. SnapFetch fetches the original stored file, which does not carry it. If a creator edited a logo or text into the video itself before uploading, that is part of the video and stays.

Can I download just the sound from a TikTok?

Yes. After pasting the link, choose the MP3 option in the Audio section and only the audio will be saved.

Do I need a TikTok account to use SnapFetch?

No. You do not need to sign in to TikTok or to SnapFetch. You only need the link to a public video.

Is there a limit on downloads?

There is no account and no daily limit from us. Very large numbers of requests in a short time may be slowed down to keep the tool fast for everyone.

Is it allowed to download TikTok videos?

Downloading is fine for your own videos and for content you have permission to save, for personal use. Videos belong to their creators — please do not re-upload someone else's work as your own. Our Terms of Use explain this in more detail.
